EST. 2026 — GRID MECHANICS

How The Grid Works

The Grid is the official ranking engine for the Inter-School Chess League. Standings update after each monthly tournament fixture.

♟

Scoring System

  • Win: +1 Points
  • Draw: +0.5 Point
  • Loss: +0 Points

Points accumulate across all monthly tournament rounds throughout the season. Gotta catch em all!

⏱

Match Format

  • Time Control: 7+2
  • Schedule: Monthly Fixtures
  • Venue: Rotates (Ainlay / OSA)

Location, schedules and extra information will be updated before each monthly tournament. check this out for October's tournament (Each host may vary the number of rounds but will always be a minimum of 5 rounds per fixture.)

🏆

Tiebreakers

  • 1st: Total Points
  • 2nd: Head-to-Head Record
  • 3rd: Total Wins

Top 3 players, a top Woman player and a top Non-binary player at the end of the season earn prizes. Additionally the top 3 players in each tournament will recieve a small prize (depending on the tournament host).
