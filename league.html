EST. 2026 — THE BATTLEGROUND

The Grid

Founded in 2026 as a sanctuary for strategic minds, the Harry Ainlay Chess Club has evolved into an inter-school arena uniting chess clubs across EPSB. From local tactical showdowns to monthly fixtures featuring elite rivals like Old Scona Academic, Lillian Osborne High School, Strathcona High School, Ross Sheppard Composite High School, we are building a premiere scholastic league where every move shapes the board.

EST. 2026 — GRID MECHANICS

How The Grid Works

The Grid is the official ranking engine for the Inter-School Chess League. Standings update after each monthly tournament fixture.

♟

Scoring System

  • Win: +1 Points
  • Draw: +0.5 Point
  • Loss: +0 Points

Points accumulate across all monthly tournament rounds throughout the season. Gotta catch em all!

⏱

Match Format

  • Time Control: 7+2
  • Schedule: Monthly Fixtures
  • Venue: Rotates (Ainlay / OSA)

Location, schedules and extra information will be updated before each monthly tournament. check this out for October's tournament (Each host may vary the number of rounds but will always be a minimum of 5 rounds per fixture.)

🏆

Tiebreakers

  • 1st: Total Points
  • 2nd: Head-to-Head Record
  • 3rd: Total Wins

Top 3 players, a top Woman player and a top Non-binary player at the end of the season earn prizes. Additionally the top 3 players in each tournament will recieve a small prize (depending on the tournament host).

EST. 2026 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

The Grid FAQ

Got questions about playing in The Grid? Click on a question below to expand the answer.

🕒 When is the arrival time? ▼

Registration opens at 4:00 PM and closes promptly at 4:45 PM. Please ensure you arrive and check in by 4:45 PM so pairings can be finalized for Round 1!

⚔️ Is it ELO / experience based? ▼

The Grid uses a Swiss-system pairing structure. In early rounds, initial ratings or estimated experience levels are used to match balanced opponents. As rounds progress, you are paired against players with similar tournament scores.

🎓 Can beginners join? ▼

Absolutely! All skill levels are welcome. The Grid is designed to give everyone experience playing competitive games regardless of whether you are a master or playing your first over-the-board tournament.

📝 How do I sign up for upcoming rounds? ▼

You can sign up directly via the embedded Google Registration Form on this page (note that the form remains the same for each host), through the host's Instagram(The Grid is getting its own instagram soon!), or by checking our News page for specific fixture announcements and deadlines.

🏅 What prizes can I win? ▼

Per-tournament prizes are awarded to top finishers by the host school. At the conclusion of the season, overall prizes are awarded to the Top 3 individual players, Top Woman player, and Top Non-binary player!
