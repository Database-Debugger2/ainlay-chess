EST. 2026 — GRID MECHANICS

How The Grid Works

The Grid is the official ranking engine for the Inter-School Chess League. Standings update after each monthly tournament fixture.

♟

Scoring System

  • Win: +1 Points
  • Draw: +0.5 Point
  • Loss: +0 Points

Points accumulate across all monthly tournament rounds throughout the season.

⏱

Match Format

  • Time Control: 7+2 Blitz / Rapid
  • Schedule: Monthly Fixtures
  • Venue: Rotates (Ainlay / OSA / Lillian)

Monthly notices are posted for host location updates and scheduled bye months.

🏆

Tiebreakers

  • 1st: Total Points
  • 2nd: Head-to-Head Record
  • 3rd: Total Wins

Top 3 positions at the end of the season earn prizes.
